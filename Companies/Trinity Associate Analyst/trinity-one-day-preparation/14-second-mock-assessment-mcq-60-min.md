## §14.1 Questions

### Section A — Quantitative aptitude (Q1–Q10)

**Q1.** A drug's revenue grew from ₹840 Cr to ₹1,050 Cr. The percentage increase is:
(a) 20% (b) 25% (c) 28% (d) 30%

**Q2.** Prescriptions fell 20% in one year and then rose 20% the next year. The net change over the two years is:
(a) 0% (b) −4% (c) +4% (d) −2%

**Q3.** In a ₹2,400 Cr market, branded to generic sales are in the ratio 7 : 3. The generic market value is:
(a) ₹480 Cr (b) ₹720 Cr (c) ₹840 Cr (d) ₹1,680 Cr

**Q4.** 40 reps write 120 Rx each; 60 reps write 180 Rx each. The weighted average Rx per rep is:
(a) 150 (b) 156 (c) 160 (d) 168

**Q5.** Revenue ₹500 Cr, COGS ₹200 Cr, SG&A ₹150 Cr. The operating margin is:
(a) 30% (b) 40% (c) 60% (d) 70%

**Q6.** A market grows from ₹1,000 Cr to ₹1,331 Cr in 3 years. The CAGR is:
(a) 9% (b) 10% (c) 11% (d) 12%

**Q7.** A takes 12 days for a task and B takes 24 days. Working together they finish in:
(a) 6 days (b) 8 days (c) 9 days (d) 10 days

**Q8.** A survey covers 2,500 physicians; 60% are cardiologists, of whom 45% are high prescribers. How many physicians are cardiologist high prescribers?
(a) 600 (b) 675 (c) 750 (d) 900

**Q9.** A rep drives two equal 240 km legs at 60 km/h and 80 km/h. Average speed:
(a) 68.6 km/h (b) 70 km/h (c) 72 km/h (d) 66 km/h

**Q10.** 2 of 10 physicians are early adopters. Two are selected at random without replacement. The probability that at least one is an early adopter is approximately:
(a) 20% (b) 34% (c) 38% (d) 44%

**Q14.** All reps who exceeded target received a bonus. Some reps who exceeded target did not attend training. Which conclusion(s) follow?

(a) Only I (b) Only II (c) Both (d) Neither

**Q15.** Did Brand X's market share rise from 2024 to 2025?
I. Brand X's revenue increased in 2025. II. Total market revenue increased in 2025.
(a) I alone sufficient (b) II alone sufficient (c) Both together sufficient (d) Neither sufficient

**Q16.** In a survey, no physician aware of Brand X was unaware of Brand Y. 40 physicians were unaware of Brand Y. Which must be true?
(a) All 40 were aware of Brand X (b) None of the 40 was aware of Brand X (c) Exactly 40 were aware of Brand X (d) Cannot be determined

**Q17.** "We should roll out the new dashboard to all teams because teams that used it reported 30% faster reporting." Which assumption is required?
(a) The dashboard caused the speed-up (b) All teams have internet access (c) Dashboards are cheap (d) Reporting speed is the only metric that matters

**Q18.** Five brands are ranked by share: D > B; B > A; A > C; E > D. Who is the largest and who is the smallest?
(a) E largest, C smallest (b) D largest, C smallest (c) E largest, A smallest (d) B largest, D smallest

# §14.3 Answer key with explanations

### Section A

**Q1 — (b) 25%.** (1,050 − 840)/840 = 210/840 = 0.25. *Distractor (a) 20% divides by the new value 1,050 — the most common error in the paper.*

**Q2 — (b) −4%.** 0.80 × 1.20 = 0.96. *Distractor (a) 0% assumes the changes cancel; they apply to different bases.*

**Q3 — (b) ₹720 Cr.** 2,400 × 3/10. *Distractor (d) ₹1,680 Cr is the branded side (7/10).*

**Q4 — (b) 156.** (40 × 120 + 60 × 180)/100 = (4,800 + 10,800)/100. *Distractor (a) 150 is the unweighted average — the trap.*

**Q5 — (a) 30%.** (500 − 200 − 150)/500 = 150/500. *Distractor (c) 60% is the **gross** margin (500 − 200)/500 — read the margin type.*

**Q6 — (b) 10%.** (1,331/1,000)^(1/3) − 1 = 1.1 − 1. *Distractor (c) 11% comes from dividing 33.1% by 3 (simple averaging of growth).*

**Q7 — (b) 8 days.** 1/(1/12 + 1/24) = 1/(3/24) = 8. *Distractor (a) 6 days would be the (incorrect) average of 12 and 24 halved — a common mis-solve.*

**Q8 — (b) 675.** 2,500 × 0.60 × 0.45 = 675. *Distractor (c) 750 forgets the 45% step.*

**Q9 — (a) 68.6 km/h.** 480/(4 + 3) = 68.57. *Distractor (b) 70 km/h is the simple average of the two speeds — invalid for equal distances.*

**Q10 — (c) 38%.** 1 − (8/10)(7/9) = 1 − 56/90 = 34/90 = 37.8%. *Distractor (a) 20% is the unconditional single-draw probability (2/10); (b) 34% is P(both not early adopters) confusion. Remember: without replacement changes the denominator.*

### Section B

**Q11 — (c) 38.** Differences +3, +5, +7, +9, then +11.

**Q12 — (c) 42.** n(n+1): 6 × 7.

**Q13 — (c) 95.** 47 × 2 + 1.

**Q14 — (a) Only I.** Those "some" reps exceeded target → got a bonus and did not attend training. II contradicts I.

**Q15 — (d) Neither sufficient.** Growth *direction* is not enough — you need the growth *rates*. Both could grow while the brand loses share.

**Q16 — (b) None of the 40 was aware of Brand X.** Everyone unaware of Y is not aware of X. *(c) inverts the relationship.*

**Q17 — (a)** The dashboard caused the speed-up; otherwise the recommendation is unsupported (self-selection).

**Q18 — (a) E largest, C smallest.** Chain: E > D > B > A > C.

### Section C

**Q19 — (b) 20.8%.** (2,415/2,000) − 1 = 20.75%. *Distractor (a) 9.9% is the CAGR (2-interval version) — a different, correct statistic for a different question. Read the stem.*

**Q20 — (c) West, +30.8%.** 390 → 510.

**Q21 — (b) 31.6%.** 764/2,415.

**Q22 — (b) 46.9%.** 300/640. *Distractor (a) 30.0% uses all 1,000 physicians as the denominator — the base trap.*

**Q23 — (b) B, +5 pp.** B: 22 → 27. (A lost 5 pp; C gained 3 pp.) *Note the answer is in percentage points, not percent.*

**Q24 — (b) 61–90.** Cumulative: 22 at 60 days, 40 at 90 days; the 28.5th territory lies in this bin.

### Section D

**Q25 — (b) mean 14, median 12.** Sum 70/5 = 14; sorted middle = 12.

**Q26 — (a) 3.58.** Squared deviations sum 64 → population variance 12.8 → √12.8. *Distractor (b) 4.00 is the sample sd (÷4); (c) 12.8 is the variance; (d) 2.53 is √(64/10), a mishandled denominator.*

**Q27 — (b).** The correct frequentist interpretation. *(a) is the common but technically incorrect phrasing; (c) misreads a statistic as a population statement; (d) claims certainty.*

### Section E

**Q28 — (b) 16.4%.** (180 × 1.2)/(1,200 × 1.1) = 216/1,320 = 16.36%. *Distractor (a) 15.0% ignores the growth differential.*

**Q29 — (c) 48.** (4,800 × 8)/800 = 38,400/800.

**Q30 — (b) rises 0.8%.** 0.90 × 1.12 = 1.008. *Distractor (a) 2% naively adds +12% − 10%; (d) "unchanged" is wrong because the uplift only offsets 0.8 pp above the 11.1% break-even.*
