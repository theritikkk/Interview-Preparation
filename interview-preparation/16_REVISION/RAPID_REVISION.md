## 90-Day Plan

Weeks 1-2: DSA foundations — `02_DSA/PATTERNS.md` plus building real fluency (not just recognition) on the 8 highest-frequency patterns (hashing, two pointers, sliding window, binary search, trees, graphs BFS/DFS, DP).
Weeks 3-4: `03_CS_FUNDAMENTALS/` in full depth, `01_PROGRAMMING/` for every language on your resume.
Weeks 5-6: `04_BACKEND/`, `05_FRONTEND/` — build small practice projects applying these concepts if time allows, not just reading.
Weeks 7-8: `06_DATABASES/`, `07_DISTRIBUTED_SYSTEMS/`, `08_AWS/` — this is your highest-leverage stretch given your resume; don't compress it.
Weeks 9-10: `13_PROJECT_DEEP_DIVES/` in full, cross-checked against your actual code, plus `11_SYSTEM_DESIGN/`, `12_SECURITY/`.
Weeks 11-12: `09_DEVOPS/`, `10_OBSERVABILITY/`, `15_INTERVIEW_PREP/` (machine coding practice, debugging practice, full mock rotation), `14_BEHAVIORAL/` (write out all STAR stories in full).
Final week: `16_REVISION/` — cheatsheets, weak-spot review only, no new material.

### Quick Revision
- Time-constrained: DSA patterns + your 3 projects (verified against real code) + system design fundamentals are non-negotiable minimums, in that priority order.
- More time: don't skip `07_DISTRIBUTED_SYSTEMS/` and `08_AWS/` for extra DSA grinding — your resume's actual differentiation is there, not in DSA depth beyond a solid baseline.
- Every plan ends with mock interviews and a no-new-material revision pass — don't cram new content the night before.
