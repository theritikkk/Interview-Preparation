### 1.5 Role responsibilities — CONFIRMED (TGaS Campus JD)

Primary responsibilities, paraphrased:
- Identify trends, provide insights, and **make recommendations** using survey data and market-research knowledge.
- Create, field, and report on custom surveys — typically using **Qualtrics**.
- Write survey questions that produce meaningful, reportable outputs.
- **Analyse survey results using Excel** and other analytical tools.
- Build client deliverables with custom automation tools, **PowerPoint**, and publishing tools.
- **Operationalise and improve processes** for standard data analysis/reporting using Excel, **VBA, Python**, and Microsoft Power Tools.
- Create **dynamic dashboards** with live data connections in **Power BI / Tableau**.
- Collaborate with India team and onshore stakeholders; present information simply.

Required skills, paraphrased: bachelor's in engineering/ops management/relevant field; pharma industry knowledge *preferred*; excellent written communication; strong analytical skills with **Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Power BI**; ability to **conduct statistical analysis on survey data**; attention to detail and ability to **check own work for errors**; market-research experience a plus; teamwork.

**Translation for a B.Tech IT candidate:** your Python/VBA/dashboard competence maps directly onto the "process improvement" and "dashboard" bullets. Your gap is survey statistics, Excel, and business framing.
