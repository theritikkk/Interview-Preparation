## 29.4 On-the-Day Tactical Checklist

### 1. Online Assessment (60 Mins)
- [ ] Have pen, paper, and physical calculator ready.
- [ ] Use the Three-Pass strategy: Pass 1 (easy questions in $\le 45$ s) $\rightarrow$ Pass 2 (medium calculations) $\rightarrow$ Pass 3 (hard logic/triage).
- [ ] Never leave a question blank; eliminate impossible options first.
- [ ] Check units: ₹ Cr vs. Lakhs, TRx vs. NRx, percentage points vs. percent.

### 2. Excel Practical Assessment (30–45 Mins)
- [ ] Minutes 0–4: Read all tasks; establish a separate `Clean` sheet; never edit raw source data.
- [ ] Minutes 5–12: De-duplicate, `TRIM` spaces, cast text-numbers to integers, audit blanks.
- [ ] Minutes 13–24: Build aggregations using `SUMIFS`/`COUNTIFS`; lock cell ranges with `$`.
- [ ] Minutes 25–34: Write lookups with `IFERROR`/`IFNA` fallbacks; verify zero `#N/A` errors.
- [ ] Minutes 35–40: Create PivotTable/Chart with sorted categories and an insight-led title.
- [ ] Minutes 41–45: Reconcile sub-totals to grand totals; verify cross-foot balance before saving.

### 3. Technical & Case-Study Interview (60 Mins)
- [ ] Ask 2–3 structured clarifying questions (scope, objective, decision criteria).
- [ ] State your custom MECE issue tree before touching a single number.
- [ ] Think aloud: walk the interviewer through your math step-by-step.
- [ ] Answer the "So What?": after every calculation, state the commercial business implication.
- [ ] Structure your final recommendation using EAR-KPI (Action, Evidence, Return, Risk, KPI).
- [ ] Stay calm if challenged: defend assumptions using scenario ranges, or acknowledge trade-offs.
